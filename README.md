## What is Varys?

Varys records your meetings and turns them into structured minutes — automatically. Press record, have your conversation, and get back a transcript with each speaker identified and a clean, ready-to-share summary of what was discussed and decided.

## Features

- 🎙️ **Record any meeting** — captures both the system audio and your microphone, so it works with Teams, Meet, Zoom, phone bridges or in-person conversations, no plugin or bot joining the call.
- 📝 **Automatic transcription** — the full conversation, written down.
- 🗣️ **Speaker identification** — voice profiles tell apart who said what, by name.
- 📋 **AI-generated minutes** — a structured summary: topics, decisions, action items.
- 🗂️ **Meeting history** — every recording, transcript and minute organized and searchable on your machine.
- 🔄 **Self-updating** — the app keeps itself current.

## Everything runs offline, on your machine

Varys is **local-first**: recording, transcription, speaker identification and minutes generation all run **on your own computer**. Your audio and your conversations stay on your machine — nothing is uploaded anywhere unless *you* choose to sync to your Varys Online workspace.

## ⚠️ Recording laws — read this before you record

**In some countries and states, recording a call or a conversation without informing the other participants is a crime.**

- **Always tell everyone** on the call that you are going to record it for the purpose of generating meeting minutes, and **ask for their consent**.
- **If anyone disagrees, do not record.**
- **Respect the laws of your country.** You are solely responsible for using Varys in compliance with the recording and privacy laws that apply to you and to the people you talk to.
